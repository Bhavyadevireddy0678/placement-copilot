Deloitte Placement Eligibility
# Last verified: manually compiled, Sep 2026

Students must have a minimum CGPA of 6.5 to apply for Deloitte.

Students must not have more than one standing backlog at the time of application; all backlogs must be cleared before the offer letter is issued.

Recruitment process typically consists of an online aptitude and technical assessment, a technical interview, and an HR interview.

Deloitte hires primarily for Analyst and Consulting Analyst roles, with some Technology Analyst openings for CSE/IT/CSE-AI branches.

Students must satisfy all placement cell eligibility requirements before applying.

The required CGPA and backlog criteria may vary depending on the specific Deloitte role and business unit (Consulting vs Technology).

Goldman Sachs Placement Eligibility
# Last verified: manually compiled, Sep 2026

Students must have a minimum CGPA of 7.5 to apply for Goldman Sachs.

Students must not have any active backlogs at the time of the recruitment process.

Recruitment process consists of an online technical assessment (coding + quantitative aptitude), followed by two to three technical interview rounds and a final HR round.

Goldman Sachs recruits for Technology Analyst roles primarily from CSE, CSE-AI, and IT branches.

Students must satisfy all placement cell eligibility requirements before applying.

The required CGPA and backlog criteria may vary depending on the specific division (Engineering vs Risk/Quant) within Goldman Sachs.

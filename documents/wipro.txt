Wipro Placement Eligibility
# Last verified: manually compiled, Sep 2026

Students must have a minimum CGPA of 6.0 to apply for Wipro.

Students are allowed a maximum of one active backlog at the time of the Wipro assessment, but it must be cleared before onboarding.

Recruitment process (Wipro Elite NTH) consists of an online aptitude and coding assessment followed by a technical-cum-HR interview.

Wipro primarily hires for Project Engineer roles across all engineering branches, with a separate Wipro Turbo track for candidates with prior internship or certification experience.

Students must satisfy all placement cell eligibility requirements before applying.

The required CGPA and backlog criteria may vary depending on whether the candidate applies via the standard Elite track or the Turbo track.

Google Placement Eligibility
# Last verified: manually compiled, Sep 2026

Students must have a minimum CGPA of 8.0 to apply for Google.

Students must not have any active or historical backlogs at the time of application.

Recruitment process consists of an online coding assessment, followed by two to three technical interview rounds focused on data structures, algorithms, and system design, and a final HR/googleyness round.

Google primarily recruits for Software Engineer and STEP Intern roles, restricted to CSE, CSE-AI, and IT branches.

Students must satisfy all placement cell eligibility requirements before applying.

Given the highly limited number of openings, actual selection bar is typically well above the stated minimum CGPA.

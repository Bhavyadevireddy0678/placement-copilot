Salesforce Placement Eligibility
# Last verified: manually compiled, Sep 2026

Students must have a minimum CGPA of 7.0 to apply for Salesforce.

Students must not have any active backlogs at the time of the recruitment process.

Recruitment process consists of an online assessment (coding + aptitude), followed by two technical interview rounds and one HR/values-fit round.

Salesforce primarily recruits for Associate Software Engineer roles from CSE, CSE-AI, and IT branches.

Students must satisfy all placement cell eligibility requirements before applying.

The required CGPA and backlog criteria may vary depending on the specific team (Platform vs Product) within Salesforce.

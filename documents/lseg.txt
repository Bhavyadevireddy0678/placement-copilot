LSEG (London Stock Exchange Group) Placement Eligibility
# Last verified: manually compiled, Sep 2026

Students must have a minimum CGPA of 7.5 to apply for LSEG.

Students must not have any active or historical backlogs at the time of application.

Recruitment process consists of an online technical assessment (DSA and CS fundamentals), followed by two technical interview rounds and one HR round.

LSEG primarily recruits for Software Engineer roles under CSE, CSE-AI, and IT branches, with a strong emphasis on data structures, algorithms, and problem solving.

Students must satisfy all placement cell eligibility requirements before applying.

The required CGPA and backlog criteria are stricter than most core-branch recruiters given LSEG's product-engineering focus.

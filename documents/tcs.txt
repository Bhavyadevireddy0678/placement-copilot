TCS Placement Eligibility
# Last verified: manually compiled, Sep 2026

Students must have a minimum CGPA of 6.0 to apply for TCS (TCS NQT pathway).

Students are allowed a maximum of one active backlog at the time of the TCS NQT exam, but all backlogs must be cleared before joining.

Recruitment process consists of the TCS National Qualifier Test (NQT) followed by a technical and HR interview round.

TCS recruits under multiple bands - TCS Ninja, TCS Digital, and TCS Prime - each with different CGPA and package thresholds.

Students must satisfy all placement cell eligibility requirements before applying.

The required CGPA and backlog criteria vary significantly by band (Ninja vs Digital vs Prime); Digital and Prime generally require CGPA of 7.0 and above.

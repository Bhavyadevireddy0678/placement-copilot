Accenture Placement Eligibility
# Last verified: manually compiled, Sep 2026

Students must have a minimum CGPA of 6.5 to apply for Accenture.

Students must not have any active backlogs at the time of the recruitment process.

Recruitment process includes an online cognitive and technical assessment, a communication assessment, and a technical/HR interview.

Accenture primarily hires for Associate Software Engineer and Application Development Associate roles across CSE, CSE-AI, IT, and ECE branches.

Students must satisfy all placement cell eligibility requirements before applying.

The required CGPA and backlog criteria may vary depending on the specific Accenture package band (standard vs advanced).

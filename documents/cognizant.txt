Cognizant Placement Eligibility
# Last verified: manually compiled, Sep 2026

Students must have a minimum CGPA of 6.0 to apply for Cognizant (GenC pathway).

Students must not have more than one active backlog at the time of the recruitment process.

Recruitment process consists of the Cognizant GenC online assessment followed by a technical and HR interview round.

Cognizant hires under multiple bands - GenC, GenC Elevate, and GenC Next - with GenC Next offering the highest package and requiring stronger DSA and project fundamentals.

Students must satisfy all placement cell eligibility requirements before applying.

The required CGPA and backlog criteria vary by GenC band; GenC Next typically requires CGPA of 7.0 and above.

Adobe Placement Eligibility
# Last verified: manually compiled, Sep 2026

Students must have a minimum CGPA of 7.5 to apply for Adobe.

Students must not have any active or historical backlogs at the time of application.

Recruitment process consists of an online coding assessment followed by two technical interview rounds and one HR round, with strong emphasis on DSA and CS fundamentals.

Adobe primarily hires for Member of Technical Staff (MTS) roles, restricted to CSE, CSE-AI, and IT branches.

Students must satisfy all placement cell eligibility requirements before applying.

The required CGPA and backlog criteria are among the stricter cutoffs on campus given the limited headcount.
